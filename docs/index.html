The core idea

One reused keystream. Two exposed messages.

When two plaintexts are encrypted with the same XOR key, XORing their ciphertexts cancels the key and reveals the XOR of the plaintexts. A guessed fragment—a crib—can then be tested at different positions.

C₁ ⊕ C₂ = (M₁ ⊕ K) ⊕ (M₂ ⊕ K) = M₁ ⊕ M₂

Attack workflow

From ciphertext to readable text

STEP 01 Collect C₁, C₂ Both were encrypted with the same key.
STEP 02 Combine Compute Z = C₁ ⊕ C₂.
STEP 03 Guess a crib Try a likely word or phrase.
STEP 04 Slide and inspect Test each valid byte offset.
STEP 05 Recover bytes Use K = C ⊕ M where plaintext is known.
